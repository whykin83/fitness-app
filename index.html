13-Week Phase Plan

Weeks 1–4 · Adapt

Start at 2,200 kcal. Let your body adjust to the deficit and new training stimulus. Hit protein every day. Deload on Week 4.

Weeks 5–8 · Accelerate

Drop to 2,000 kcal. This is the main fat-loss block. Scale cardio to 2× tennis sessions or 30-min walks on rest days. Week 8 deload.

Weeks 9–13 · Final Push

Hold 2,000 kcal or drop to 1,800 if progress stalls. Add tennis-specific footwork drills. Week 12 deload. Week 13: peak week.

Rest Day Eating

Drop to 1,700–1,800 kcal on non-training days. Keep protein the same (178g). Reduce carbs to 100–120g. No need to eat back calories.
