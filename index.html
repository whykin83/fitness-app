Your 3-month mission
94kg → 83kg in 13 weeks. That's 11kg, requiring an average deficit of ~935 kcal/day. Aggressive but achievable with 3 gym sessions/week, daily activity, and strict protein adherence. Your athletic background gives you a higher muscle base — fat loss will be faster than for a sedentary person of the same weight.

Protein logic. 1.9g × 94kg = 178g/day. This is the minimum to preserve muscle mass during an aggressive cut. On days you miss the target, your body has no choice but to break down muscle for energy — this is why protein is the non-negotiable macro.
13-week phase plan
Weeks 1–4 · Foundation cut
Strict 2,000 kcal. 178g protein daily. Body adapts to caloric deficit. Expect 0.8–1.2kg/week loss. Weight may fluctuate first 2 weeks due to water and glycogen.
Weeks 5–8 · Momentum phase
Same calorie target. Add carb cycling on workout days (+30g carbs pre/post gym, remove from non-training days). Fat loss accelerates as body adapts.
Weeks 9–13 · Final push
If stalling (no loss for 10+ days), reduce by 150 kcal. Keep protein at 178g. This is where you cross 85kg territory. Tennis fitness peaks here.
Every 4th week · Diet break
Eat at maintenance (~2,953 kcal) for 7 days. Hormones reset, muscle synthesis increases. This is not cheating — it's strategy. You will come back leaner.
Eating out rules (Malaysia context)
Choose protein first. Double chicken at nasi campur, extra fish at hawker stalls. The protein decision drives everything else.
Skip sauces and gravies where possible — they are where the hidden calories live. Ask for rice on the side so you control the portion.
Drink only water, black coffee, or teh-o-kosong. Teh tarik is 150+ kcal per glass. Three drinks/day = 450 hidden calories.
On gym days: eat your highest-carb meal 1–2 hours pre-workout. Rice or bread before training, not before bed.

Weigh-in log
Weigh-in rules for accuracy
Same time every day — Sunday morning works best. Post-bathroom, pre-breakfast. Wear the same amount of clothing. Weight fluctuates 1–2kg daily from water, food, and sodium. The trend over 7–10 days is what matters — not any single reading.

Progressive overload — double progression

01
Work within prescribed rep range (e.g., 8–12)
02
Hit the top of the range for 2 consecutive sessions → add weight
03
Compounds +2.5kg, isolation +1kg. Drop back to bottom of rep range
04
Repeat. This rule drives 80% of physique progress
RPE guide
7
Could do 3+ more reps. Too easy — add weight or reduce rest time.
8
Could do 2 more reps. The sweet spot for hypertrophy stimulus.
9
Could do 1 more rep. Reserve for compound lifts only.
10
Absolute failure. Avoid at 43 — recovery cost is too high.

Non-negotiables
Log every session. Weight used, reps completed, RPE. Without a log, progressive overload is guesswork.
178g protein daily. Non-negotiable on training days. This is what builds the physique — the gym is just the trigger.
7–8 hours sleep. Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep. Training without sleep is negative ROI.
Never skip deload week. At 43, accumulated fatigue is invisible. The deload is where supercompensation happens. Skipping it costs you 2 weeks of gains.
